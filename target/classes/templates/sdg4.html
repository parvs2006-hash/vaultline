🛠️ How EduTrack Implements SDG 4 Principles

🔍

1. Early Warning & Retention

Low attendance and failing marks are primary leading indicators of student disengagement. EduTrack flags students the moment their attendance dips below 75% or scores fall below 40%, preventing dropouts before exam week.

📈

2. Data-Driven Pedagogy

Teachers receive instant subject-wise average analytics. If a class exhibits consistently low performance in Java or Mathematics, faculty can organize timely remedial classes and adjust teaching strategies.

🎯

3. Transparent Student Mentoring

Students obtain transparent, rule-based academic feedback and actionable suggestions directly within their personal portal, eliminating anxiety and encouraging proactive communication with faculty advisors.
